Ara UNO R3 pot, fit-test v5

Test the power plug with the short rear wall first

Import base_cover.stl and rear_port_test.stl in millimetres at 100 percent.
Both files are already oriented flat on the print bed. Use the v5 base
with the v5 wall. The short wall copies the full pot's power opening.

1. Place the UNO flat on the four posts, with its connectors towards the
   nearest edge of the base.
   Check all four mounting holes before fastening. The posts have 2.1 mm
   blind pilot holes for short M2.5 thread-forming screws for plastic.
   Check screw depth and underside clearance on the real board.
2. Seat the short rear wall on the base and turn the wall until you can
   see the barrel jack through the hole. The plug enters at a slight angle.
   Check that it fully engages without rubbing. Avoid forcing the plug.
3. If the fit is good, print body.stl and inner_tray.stl. The separate
   rim_clip_test.stl is optional for checking the bird clasp or rim bridges.

Full assembly

The outside dimensions are 118 mm across the rim, 110 mm across the base
and 88 mm tall including the 3 mm base plate. The four 36 x 6 mm clip slots
and the 5 mm gripping strip above them are unchanged. The cover seats on
a round lip. Use removable tape to hold the base in alignment for testing.

The UNO sits horizontally on 6 mm posts. Lower the body over the base
with the power cable disconnected and the loose leads tucked inside.
The 12 mm power bore sits beneath the rear clip slot and angles toward
the barrel jack. Rotate the shell on the base as you did with the test
wall, then plug in. The CAD check used an 11 mm diameter straight plug
body. Check your plug with the test wall before printing the full pot.

Use thin double-sided mounting tape at the housing edges to secure the
speaker under the tray, with its sound-emitting face towards the five
grille slots. Keep tape clear of the cone, wires and slots. Drop the tray
and speaker assembly onto the inner ledge after connecting the leads.
Secure the RTC separately using an insulated holder or pad in the reserved
space opposite the power connectors.

Print settings

Use standard FDM, 0.2 mm layers as a starting point, and any stock colour.
Print pieces separately in their supplied orientation. Use the slicer's
layer preview to check bridges over the rim slots and the power opening.
The wide rim slots may need local removable support. A brim can help the
short curved test wall stay on the bed. This model has not been sliced
or physically printed for this revision.

Design basis and checks

The photos identify the board as ELEGOO UNO R3. The quoted 129 x 95 x 19 mm
dimensions match the retail package dimensions, not the bare board outline.
The layout uses the Arduino UNO R3 reference footprint, about 69 x 53 mm,
with extra space for protruding ports, headers and wires. Verify the actual
clone's hole positions and connectors with the two-piece test.
Arduino reference CAD: https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/uno-rev3/
Speaker supplied dimensions: 70 x 31 x 16 mm. Space reserved for its housing,
mounting tape and tolerance: 72 x 33 x 19 mm. RTC reserve: 34 x 26 mm upright,
11 mm thick, provisional. Acoustics and physical fit remain unverified.
