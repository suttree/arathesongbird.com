Ara tapered plant pot, test-print v7

Print the two small snap samples first

Print snap_body_test.stl and snap_base_test.stl separately at 100 percent,
in millimetres, with their supplied flat faces on the bed. Together they
copy one full clip, the local base lip and the matching curved body wall.
Use the same printer, material and settings intended for the full parts.

Place the curved wall on the sample base, with its opening facing the hook.
Press the wall down over the sloping hook until the joint clicks and seats.
Check that the pieces resist a gentle pull apart. Press the hook inward
through the side opening with a small blunt flat tool, then lift the wall.
Repeat a few times and inspect the root for whitening, splitting or cracks.
If it is too tight, loose or brittle, adjust and reprint the sample before
ordering the full enclosure. Leave the power disconnected during assembly.

Full parts to print

Print body.stl, base_cover.stl and inner_tray.stl as a matched v7 set.
The rim is 132 mm across, the base is 110 mm across and the assembled
height is 88 mm. The side taper is 7.37 degrees from vertical.
The base diameter preserves the clearance needed to lower the shell
over the UNO. The wider rim and resized tray create the stronger taper.
Use the v7 snap samples, which copy the new sloping wall and spring arms.

Two upright clips on opposite sides of the cover engage small rectangular
openings in the body. Each clip is 8 mm wide and 1.2 mm thick, with a
rounded inner root and a ramped hook. The arms lean outward with the
wall. The nominal radial engagement is
0.6 mm. The joint allows 0.5 mm on either side of each hook and 0.3 mm
above and below it. The locating ring has gaps around the spring arms.

Fit the UNO to the four posts on the bottom cover as on v5. The board
mounts still use 2.1 mm pilot holes for short M2.5 plastic screws.
Check screw depth and clearance beneath the board before fastening.
The snap clips secure the bottom cover to the body.

Align the body's power opening with the UNO barrel jack before pressing
the body onto the cover. The two clips allow two opposing orientations,
so confirm that you can see the jack through the power opening first.
Press near the two clips until both click and the bottom seam sits flush.
Check the power plug seats fully, then install the speaker and tray.

To open, disconnect power and hold the enclosure over a table. Depress
both hooks through the side openings using small blunt flat tools while
gently easing the cover downward. Keep tools against the plastic hooks.
Avoid twisting the cover while either clip remains engaged.

Speaker and tray

The tray has five grille slots. Secure the speaker housing under it with
thin double-sided mounting tape, keeping the cone and grille clear.
The speaker reserve is 72 x 33 x 19 mm around the stated 70 x 31 x 16 mm
housing. The provisional RTC reserve is upright opposite the connectors.

Print settings

PETG is the suggested first material for the clips. Ask the printer to
print both samples in PETG first. PLA clip flexibility needs a sample
test with the actual filament. Snap life depends on material and layer
bonding, especially with these upright arms.

Start with a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers and at least three perimeter
walls. Ask the printer to check that the 1.2 mm beams print as continuous
walls. Check the slicer preview around the roots, hook ramps and side
openings. Keep supports out of the flexible arm gaps. The 36 mm rim slots
may need local removable support. A brim may help the narrow sample wall.

Validation and limits

The CAD checks cover watertight meshes, component clearance, plug access,
hook engagement, release clearance and the insertion stroke with
inward-deflected clip envelopes. A simple beam calculation estimates
nominal strain of about 1.54 percent, and does not establish real printed
strength or force. The increased taper requires more clip deflection
during insertion, so test the new samples even if a v6 sample passed.
Clip force, repeated opening and physical snap fit require the samples.
This revision has not been sliced or physically printed.

The v5 electronics layout follows the UNO R3 reference footprint, with
space for wires and protruding connectors. The 12 mm angled power bore
has an unobstructed path for the checked 11 mm diameter straight plug.

Other optional samples

rear_port_test.stl copies the power opening, and can be used with the
full base to check cable access. It covers the rear wall only.
rim_clip_test.stl checks the bird clasp and the rim slot bridge.
